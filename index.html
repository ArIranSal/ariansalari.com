FIG. 02 — ABOUT

Background, path, and interests.

I'm a mechanical engineering student at the University of California, Irvine, driven by a long-standing curiosity about how things work and how ideas can be transformed into something real. From an early age, I was drawn to creating, experimenting, and building, often using science fairs as opportunities to explore new ideas and turn questions into hands-on projects. Over time, that curiosity grew into a deeper passion for engineering and for understanding how physical systems can be designed, improved, and brought to life.

My path into engineering developed from those early projects into a broader interest in mechanical systems and the integration of modern technology and artificial intelligence into engineering. At Irvine Valley College, I strengthened my foundation through relevant engineering coursework while also pursuing research focused on applying emerging technologies and AI to engineering problems. I later transferred to UC Irvine to continue building on that foundation and further develop my understanding of mechanical engineering.

Today, I'm especially interested in the intersection of mechanical engineering, autonomous technology, and artificial intelligence. I'm fascinated by how intelligent software can work alongside physical hardware to create systems that perceive their surroundings, adapt, and make decisions autonomously. What excites me most is the process of taking an ambitious idea from imagination and transforming it into a functional, engineered system.
